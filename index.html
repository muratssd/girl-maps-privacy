Privacy Policy

Girl Maps is a navigation app for iPhone, Apple Watch, and CarPlay. This policy explains what information is needed to provide maps, routes, subscriptions, and app reliability, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Information Girl Maps uses

  • Location. When you grant permission, Girl Maps uses your precise location to show your position, search nearby, calculate routes, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and continue an active trip in the background. Girl Maps does not request Always Location access.
  • Search and route information. Destinations, search queries, route requests, and navigation progress are processed as needed to provide mapping and navigation features.
  • Purchases. Apple and our subscription analytics provider process product identifiers, subscription status, and transaction information needed to complete, restore, and measure purchases. Girl Maps never receives your full payment card details.
  • App diagnostics and usage. Limited technical events may be used to understand reliability, onboarding, and subscription performance. These events do not include precise location, search text, place names, or route geometry.
  • Preferences stored on your device. Settings such as the selected map world, voice, saved places, and onboarding state are stored locally so the app works as expected.

Services that support the app

Girl Maps uses established service providers only for the functions described below. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy policies.

Mapbox

Maps, place search, routing, traffic-aware navigation, and related map services.

Apple

App distribution, StoreKit subscriptions, purchase history, and system services.

Adapty

Subscription analytics in observer mode. Girl Maps disables advertising ID, attribution, and IP-address collection in Adapty.

Firebase

Privacy-conscious app analytics and remote configuration without advertising personalization.

How information is used

Information is used to:

  • provide maps, search, routes, navigation, voice guidance, Watch, and CarPlay;
  • verify Premium access and restore subscriptions;
  • maintain app security, reliability, and performance;
  • understand whether essential app and subscription flows work correctly; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect users and the service.

Girl Maps does not sell personal information and does not use your precise location, destinations, or routes for advertising.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared with service providers only when needed to operate the features above, process purchases, protect the service, or comply with law. Girl Maps does not create a user account or maintain a developer-operated profile containing your trip history. Local preferences remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Service providers retain information according to their policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Change location access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Stop background location use by ending active navigation.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

Children

Girl Maps is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

International processing and security

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Girl Maps, its providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a request in the Girl Maps privacy support repository. Please do not include precise locations, routes, payment details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
